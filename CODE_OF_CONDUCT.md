# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, constructive community.

Be patient and respectful, welcome contributors of all experience levels, protect privacy, and discuss disagreements in good faith. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and abusive conduct are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially to the extent possible. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.
